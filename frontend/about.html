Surplus food should feed people, not fill bins

FoodBridge is a platform that connects food that would be thrown away with the people who need it, before it spoils.

Why we built it

Hotels, caterers and event halls often end the day with good food left over. NGOs and shelters often struggle to get fresh meals. The gap between them is time: food that is safe now is not safe in a few hours, and phone calls take too long.

FoodBridge closes that gap with one shared countdown. A donor posts, a verified NGO accepts, and a volunteer delivers, and everyone can see how much safe time is left. It supports the UN goal of Zero Hunger (SDG 2).

What we stand for

Safety first

Every donation gets a deadline. Late food is closed automatically and cannot be handed over.

Trust

NGOs are checked by an admin before they can accept food.

Privacy

Addresses and phone numbers are shared only with the people in a pickup. Other people see an approximate area.

Built with five agents

A Donor Agent, Safety Agent, Matching Agent, Logistics Agent and Impact Agent each do one clear job, so nobody has to coordinate by phone.
