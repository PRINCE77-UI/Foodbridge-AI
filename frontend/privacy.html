Privacy Policy

This is a plain-language template written for a student project. Have it reviewed by a legal professional before a public launch.

What we collect

Your name or organisation, mobile number, area, optional location (only if you allow it in your browser), NGO registration number (NGOs only), and the food donations you post or handle.

Why we collect it

To connect donors, NGOs and volunteers, to show distance to a pickup, to send in-app alerts, and to count the total meals rescued.

Who can see it

Your exact pickup address is shown only to the donor, NGO and volunteer involved in that pickup. Other users see only the food details, the distance and an approximate map area (about 1 km). The platform admin can see all accounts and donations, including phone numbers and addresses, to verify NGOs, keep the service safe and give support. We do not sell your data or show ads.

Maps

Maps are shown with OpenStreetMap. Your browser loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address like any website you visit.

Your passwords

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. We cannot read them.

Your choices

You can turn off location in your browser at any time. To delete your account and data, contact us at the email below.
