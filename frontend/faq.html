Questions, answered

Short answers about safety, privacy and how FoodBridge works.

About the service

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no fee to post, accept or deliver food.

What food can I donate?

Fresh cooked food, and packaged or raw food. Cooked food can be offered for up to 4 hours. Packaged or raw food can be offered for the hours you set, up to 12.

How is the safe time decided?

You tell us how long the food stays safe. The Safety Agent keeps 20 minutes aside for handling and sets the delivery deadline. If there is not enough time left, the donation is not accepted.

What happens if nobody collects the food in time?

It is marked expired and everyone involved gets an alert. Expired food should not be served.

Can I cancel a donation?

Yes, while it is still waiting for an NGO. A volunteer can also give a pickup back if they cannot go.

Trust and privacy

Can any NGO accept food?

No. An admin checks the registration number of each NGO before it can accept.

Who can see my address and phone number?

Only the donor, the NGO and the volunteer in that pickup. The platform admin can also see it, to verify NGOs, keep the service safe and give support.

What do other people see on the map?

An approximate area of about 1 km. The exact spot is shown only after an NGO accepts and only to the people in that pickup.

How are alerts sent?

Alerts go only to verified NGOs and volunteers within 30 km of the pickup.

How do I delete my account?

Write to us at the email address in the footer of the home page and we will remove your account and data.

Using the app

Do I need a vehicle to volunteer?

No. Anyone who can deliver nearby can volunteer. The directions button helps you reach the pickup and the NGO.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in your phone browser and choose Add to Home Screen to install it like an app.
