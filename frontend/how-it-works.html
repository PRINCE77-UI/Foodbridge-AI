From leftover food to a full plate, in one tracked journey

Every donation has a countdown. Each person in the chain sees what to do next and how much time is left.

Pick your role

Each role has a short list of things to do.

Donors (hotels, caterers, canteens)

  1. Sign up and allow your location.
  2. Tap Donate. Enter the food, the meals and how long it stays safe.
  3. Drop the pin on the map where the food can be collected.
  4. Watch it move from accepted to picked up to delivered.

NGOs and shelters

  1. Sign up with your registration number.
  2. Wait for the admin to verify your NGO.
  3. Open Requests. Food nearest to you is shown first.
  4. Accept what you can serve. A volunteer is alerted straight away.

Volunteers

  1. Sign up and allow your location.
  2. Open Pickups to see accepted food that needs a rider.
  3. Take a pickup. Use the directions button to reach the donor, then the NGO.
  4. Mark it picked up, then delivered.

The journey of one donation

Four stages, each one visible to everyone involved.

  • PostedThe Safety Agent sets a deadline. Cooked food is limited to 4 hours, and 20 minutes are kept aside for handling.
  • NGO acceptedOnly verified NGOs can accept, and only food that matches their diet rule. The first NGO to accept gets it.
  • Picked upA volunteer takes the pickup. The exact address and phone numbers are now shared with the people in this pickup.
  • DeliveredThe donor and the NGO are told, and the meals are added to the impact numbers.

If something goes wrong

Time runs out

The donation is marked expired and everyone involved is alerted. Expired food should not be served.

A volunteer cannot go

They can give the pickup back. Nearby volunteers are alerted again.
