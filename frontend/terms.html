What FoodBridge is

FoodBridge is a platform that connects food donors, NGOs and volunteers. We do not cook, store, buy or sell food and we are not a party to the donation.

Food safety

Donors must only post food that is fresh, hygienically prepared and safe to eat, and must give honest information about type, diet and safe time. The safety timer is a guide, not a guarantee. NGOs may refuse any food they consider unsafe.

NGO verification

NGOs must be reviewed by an admin before they can accept food. Giving false registration details can lead to removal.

Volunteers

Volunteers deliver at their own choice and risk and must follow traffic rules and keep food covered and clean.

Fair use

Do not post false listings, harass others, or misuse another person's details. We may remove accounts that break these rules.

Prototype notice

This service may be a prototype and is provided as is, without warranties.
